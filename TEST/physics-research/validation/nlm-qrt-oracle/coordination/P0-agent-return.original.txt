### Trace B — PHYS-NLM-021: static-friction interval and regime switch

Observed version: bank `/questions/20`, beginning at [line 2599](https://github.com/reallakshman19/Grade9v3.5/blob/455103ad312ab8b6627aa7a1199b0bd7d5b0af8e/TEST/physics-research/question_bank_mechanics_grade9.v1.json?utm_source=chatgpt.com#L2599).

The current bank describes a 4 kg box on a 30° rough incline connected to a variable hanging mass, with \\(\mu_s=0.30\\), \\(\mu_k=0.20\\) and \\(g=10\\).

It declares `SYNTHESIZE-D4`, `NLM-04`, and rival `RETRIEVE`. The actual learner decision requires distinguishing the static admissibility range from the moving kinetic-friction regime.

A check from the explicit model gives:

\\[ N=4(10)(0.866)=34.64\ \mathrm N \\]

\\[ |20-10M|\leq 0.30(34.64) \\]

\\[ 0.9608\leq M\leq3.0392\ \mathrm{kg} \\]

For \\(M=3.20\\) kg, sliding occurs with the hanger descending:

\\[ a=\frac{32-20-0.20(34.64)}{4+3.2} \approx0.7044\ \mathrm{m/s^2}. \\]

This numerical check assumes an ideal taut cord, contact maintained and ordinary kinetic friction after motion begins. It is not independent scientific sign-off, particularly because #304 supplied the expected discriminator.
