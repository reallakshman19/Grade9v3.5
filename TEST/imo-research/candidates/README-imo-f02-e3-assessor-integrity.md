# E3-B assessor-controlled evidence — candidate protocol, not a learner release

The F02 Index Laws teaching package and its CORE1A exit are public/disclosed, so their answers cannot establish genuinely unseen independent transfer. The proposed E3-B offline workflow must be commissioned and witnessed by an independent Grade 9 mathematics assessor. First resolve the still-pending real-exponent/integer-domain/foundational curriculum choice and D1-D5 review in #130/#137.

An authorized assessor must privately hold the newly authored same-family item and answer key, preapprove the five-move reasoning rubric (positive exponential quantity; multiplying rather than adding the base; reversible substitution; recovery of the unique admissible exponent; verification in the original equation), retain pre-delivery item custody and help-disclosure history, witness the first unaided attempt, and independently review alternative solutions and misconception-vs-slip. H/S/P/M judgements and Owner rights/publication are separate.

The TEST-only verifier checks a digest-only metadata envelope. Never put private items, keys, answers, student names, free text, or raw attempts into GitHub Actions. An off-repository reviewer may use an externally controlled 32-byte-or-longer HMAC key, hex encoded as environment variable IMO_E3_EXTERNAL_ASSESSOR_KEY_HEX, and call the verifier with --private-envelope. The verifier checks metadata integrity, known disclosed-item hashes, chronology, absence of hint/answer views, and rejects forged academic status. A matching MAC merely proves metadata came from a holder of the supplied shared key. It does NOT prove reviewer independence, authenticated learner identity, true unseenness, truthful chronological facts, or academic correctness. The key must be held independently; a claimant choosing their own key creates no trust.

All verifier outputs remain fixed privacy-safe codes. Even for a matching MAC, credit_eligible, new_unseen_item_verified, mathematical_reasoning_verified and learner_release_authorized are always false; academic_status remains HOLD_NOT_ACCEPTED. The public workflow runs only synthetic falsifiers and generates an empty external-witness HOLD ledger. A real off-repo independent assessor and curriculum/Owner reviewers must adjudicate and authorize subsequent stages; no learner product is changed.
